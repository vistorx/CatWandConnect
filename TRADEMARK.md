# Trademark policy

The files of CatWandConnect are open (see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)); the **name "CatWandConnect" and its logo** are not. They identify the project and the official kits made by its creator, so people know what they are getting. This policy explains what you can and cannot do with them. It is written in plain language and is not a substitute for the licences.

## You can, without asking

- Build a CatWandConnect for yourself, your friends or your cat, and call it a CatWandConnect.
- Talk about the project, write reviews, tutorials and videos, and use the name to refer to it.
- Fork the repository and keep the name in the code and docs, as long as your fork stays clearly a fork (for example in its README).
- Say that your product, add-on, firmware or part is **"compatible with CatWandConnect"** or **"based on CatWandConnect"**, if that is true, in a way that does not suggest it is official.

## You need another name to

- Sell assembled robots, kits or parts built from these designs. That is allowed by the licences, but call your product something else, for example "WhiskerBot (compatible with CatWandConnect)".
- Distribute modified firmware to others as a product or a ready-made binary. Rename it, so bug reports don't land on the wrong project.
- Use the name or logo in a product name, company name, domain name, social media account or app name.
- Use the logo in any way that suggests endorsement.

## Official kits

Only kits sold by the project's creator may be called "CatWandConnect" or "official CatWandConnect". If you would like to sell under the name, for example as a partner or reseller, open an issue or contact the maintainer to ask.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether your use is fine, ask in an issue. We are happy to say yes to most uses that are honest about where things come from.
