# Licensing

CatWandConnect is fully open. Each kind of file has the licence that fits it, and all of them let anyone build, modify, share and sell. See [ADR 0006](docs/adr/0006-open-licences-reserved-name.md) for the reasoning.

| What | Where | Licence |
|------|-------|---------|
| Firmware and tools | `firmware/` | [GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSES/GPL-3.0-or-later.txt) |
| Hardware designs (STL, 3MF and CAD sources) | `stls/`, `cads/` | [CERN-OHL-S-2.0](LICENSES/CERN-OHL-S-2.0.txt) |
| Documentation, images and everything else | `docs/`, `media/`, the READMEs and other text | [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt) |

Copyright © 2026 vistorx and the CatWandConnect contributors.

All three are "share-alike" licences:

- If you distribute modified firmware, publish its source under the GPL.
- If you make or sell hardware based on these designs, share your modified design files under CERN-OHL-S.
- If you republish adapted documentation, keep it under CC BY-SA and credit the project.

The licences cover the files, not the name. The name "CatWandConnect" and its logo (in `media/logo/`) are covered by the [trademark policy](TRADEMARK.md): you may build and sell your own version, but under another name.

Contributions are accepted under the licence of the files they change.
