CatWandConnect

Install the cat toy robot's firmware on an ESP32, straight from your browser.

Beta versions get the newest changes first and may still have bugs.

Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over HTTPS.

Before you start

  • Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS).
  • An ESP32, ESP32-S3 or ESP32-C3 board and a USB cable that carries data, not only power. ESP8266 boards are not supported.
  • If the board doesn't show up, install its USB driver: CH340/CH9102 (WCH) or CP210x (Silicon Labs), depending on the chip next to its USB port.

Steps

  1. Plug the board into the computer, then press the button above and pick its port.
  2. Choose "Install CatWandConnect". The right firmware for your chip is picked automatically. Erase the board on a first install.
  3. When it's done, choose "Connect to Wi-Fi" and enter your Wi-Fi name and password.
  4. Choose "Visit device" to open the robot's interface. Next time, it's at http://catwandconnect.local/.

Skipped Wi-Fi? The robot opens its own CatWandConnect-XXXX hotspot with a setup page.
