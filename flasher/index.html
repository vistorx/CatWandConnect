CatWandConnect

Install the cat toy robot's firmware on an ESP32, straight from your browser.

Beta versions get the newest changes first and may still have bugs.

Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over HTTPS.

Before you start

  • Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS).
  • An ESP32, ESP32-S3 or ESP32-C3 board and a USB cable that carries data, not only power. ESP8266 boards are not supported.
  • If the board doesn't show up, try another cable, then install the driver for the USB chip next to its connector (help below).
The board doesn't show up?
  1. Try another USB cable: many only carry power. Plug straight into the computer, not through a hub.
  2. Read the marking on the small chip next to the USB connector, and install its driver:
ChipWindowsmacOS
CH340, CH341 CH341SER.EXE CH34XSER_MAC.ZIP
CH9102, CH343 CH343SER.EXE CH34XSER_MAC.ZIP
CP2102, CP210x Silicon Labs CP210x drivers
  1. Unplug and replug the board, then press the button again. On macOS, allow the driver in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. No chip next to the connector (ESP32-S3 or ESP32-C3 with native USB)? No driver is needed: hold the BOOT button while plugging the board in.

Linux and ChromeOS need no driver.

More help: troubleshooting guide.

Still stuck? Ask on Discord (no GitHub account needed) or see Getting help.

Steps

  1. Plug the board into the computer, then press the button above and pick its port.
  2. Choose "Install CatWandConnect". The right firmware for your chip is picked automatically. Erase the board on a first install.
  3. When it's done, choose "Connect to Wi-Fi" and enter your Wi-Fi name and password.
  4. Choose "Visit device" to open the robot's interface. Note its address, for example http://catwandconnect-3f9a.local/, to open it next time.

Skipped Wi-Fi? The robot opens its own CatWandConnect-XXXX hotspot with a setup page.
